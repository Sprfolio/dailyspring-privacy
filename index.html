DailySpring Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Information stored on your device

DailySpring stores the task names, schedule times, categories, dates, and completion status you enter in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not require an account and does not operate a server for your schedule data.

Collection and sharing

SPRFOLIO does not collect or receive the schedule information you enter in DailySpring, and the app does not share it with other companies. DailySpring does not include advertising or analytics services.

Reminders

DailySpring schedules reminders and task deadlines locally on your device. It may request Android notification permission so the system can display task reminders. You can change notification permission in Android settings.

Device backup

Android may include app data in device backups if backup is enabled in your device or Google account settings. Those backups are managed by Android and Google, not by SPRFOLIO. Backup availability, retention, and deletion controls depend on your device and account settings.

Retention and deletion

Schedule data remains in the app until you clear DailySpring's storage or uninstall the app. If Android or Google has backed up app data, that backup follows the platform's own retention and deletion controls.

Children

DailySpring is a general-purpose schedule and task tracking app. It does not ask users to provide personal information or create an account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The current version will be available at the public URL where this policy is hosted.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact SPRFOLIO at thesprfolio@gmail.com.
